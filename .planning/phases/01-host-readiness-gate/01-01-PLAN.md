### Final V1 envelope contract

`G0EvidenceEnvelopeV1` contains only these strict top-level members:
`schema`, `version`, `base`, and `extensions`. `base` is final here and
contains schema/run provenance, run/boot/session identity, exact argv,
executing-binary digest, wall and monotonic start/finish timestamps, overall
gate status, and stable ordered reasons. Unknown or duplicate top-level/base
members fail.

`extensions` contains at most 16 unique `G0ExtensionRecordV1` values. Each
record has a stable ASCII identifier, extension schema version, terminal
status, bounded raw JSON payload, and SHA-256 of those exact raw payload bytes.
Each raw payload is at most 256 KiB and the entire input is at most 1 MiB.
Unknown well-formed extension identifiers are preserved byte-for-byte and do
not fail the base decoder merely because their semantics are unknown. A gate
that owns a known identifier validates that payload separately and strictly.

The initial known identifiers are `host-foundation.v1`,
`selected-output.v1`, `nvfbc-capture.v1`, and `nvenc-tuples.v1`. Plan 01-01
defines their names and generic record semantics only; later plans add their
owned payload validators without mutating V1 base decoding.
