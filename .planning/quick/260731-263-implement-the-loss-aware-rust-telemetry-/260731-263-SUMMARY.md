## Decisions Made

- A CUDA copy error ends the capture path without publishing evidence; `acquired` therefore means the application-owned same-GPU surface is ready.
- `last_accepted_sequence` uses atomic `fetch_max` after `try_send` succeeds, preventing concurrent callbacks from regressing the terminal maximum.
- The bounded callback channel owns normal batches only. Terminal state bypasses that channel and is marked reported only after MessagePack serialization and KyCom send succeed.
- Terminal transport success is not remote receipt proof; the later consumer must require the terminal record itself.
